AUDIO & IN-GAME ADS

REBUILD
WHAT SURVIVES
The hands-in-frame device — a real person holding a real screen. The calm neutral ground and the light hairline grid. The interface itself is legible at slide size.
WHAT FAILS
The name promises two worlds and the image delivers neither: no game, and the music player is a generic streaming clone. Flat studio light, no time of day, no place. The ad unit reads as a display banner that happens to sit in an audio app.
THE REBUILD
Commit to audio: night street, headphones on, phone held low, screen legible at a glance, city light on the face. The waveform is the only neon element. Build in-game as its own scene — controller, screen glow, a hoarding inside the game world.

FORMAT 02 / 08IMAGE AUDIT03 / 10

DOOH

KEEP
WHAT SURVIVES
Everything structural. Scale, silhouettes, a single light source, the reflection on the floor. It is the only image in the set that already reads as photography rather than a mockup.
WHAT FAILS
The pale yellow field washes out and dilutes the accent — the screen should be near-white with the accent as a detail. The type on the screen is set in a different family from the rest of the set. The mirrored floor lettering is a gimmick; keep the reflection, drop the readable text in it.
THE REBUILD
Same scene, colder ambient, screen content rebuilt in the type system. One passer-by mid-stride, cropped at the edge, for scale. Shoot the plate clean so the screen can be re-dressed for any client.

CONNECTED TV

REPLACE
WHAT SURVIVES
Almost nothing. The three-quarter angle on the panel is the one usable idea.
WHAT FAILS
Netflix, Disney+, Roku and Spotify are doing the selling — borrowed credibility and a real legal exposure. The set is a grey render floating on black with no room, no sofa, no viewer, no light from the screen falling on anything.
THE REBUILD
A living room at dusk, screen light on faces and walls, people watching rather than posing. The interface is ours: a neutral, believable streaming shell with the ad in the real slot. Detail shot of the remote as a second frame.

DYNAMIC ADS

REBUILD
WHAT SURVIVES
The two-device pairing — the format genuinely needs to show more than one state at once.
WHAT FAILS
Two phones floating on beige is a downloaded mockup, and nothing in it is dynamic: both screens say the same thing. The category is about the message changing by audience, weather, time or stock, and the image never shows a variation.
THE REBUILD
One placement, three states, same frame: the slot re-rendering for a different city, hour and offer. Physical staging — phone on a surface with real light — or a locked-off frame with the screen swapping. The change is the subject.

GEOFENCING

REBUILD
WHAT SURVIVES
The photography. Hard directional light on concrete, an honest shadow, the phone resting in a real place — the most believable staging in the whole set.
WHAT FAILS
Ducati is doing the selling again. The map is a stock illustration, the red pin is off-system, and the geofence itself — the boundary, the radius, the trigger — is never shown.
THE REBUILD
Keep the concrete and the light. Rebuild the screen: a neutral dark map, our own advertiser, and the fence drawn as a radius the eye can read. Show the trigger moment, not a static listing.

FORMAT 06 / 08IMAGE AUDIT07 / 10

DISPLAY ADS

REBUILD
WHAT SURVIVES
The format ladder — showing the same creative across skyscraper, half-page, leaderboard and billboard is exactly what a buyer needs to see.
WHAT FAILS
It is a diagram, not an image. Yellow type on mid-tone photography loses contrast at every size, and the crops are automatic — the same picture squeezed into five boxes with the focal point lost in four of them.
THE REBUILD
Photograph one strong scene, then compose each size individually so the subject survives the crop. Sizes stay in a clean neutral field with real proportions and a mono size label under each.

FORMAT 07 / 08IMAGE AUDIT08 / 10

NATIVE ADS

REBUILD
WHAT SURVIVES
The explanatory frame is the best in the set: a real editorial page with the sponsored unit sitting inside the feed, marked as sponsored. That is exactly what native has to prove.
WHAT FAILS
The laptop floats on black with a fake shadow. The portal is invented and inconsistent, the grey placeholder blocks read as unfinished, and the yellow panel is a hole rather than a piece of content.
THE REBUILD
Desk, daylight, hands near the trackpad, screen photographed straight and re-dressed in post. Two frames: the unit in the feed, and the article it opens into. Sponsored labelling always legible.

FORMAT 08 / 08IMAGE AUDIT09 / 10

RICH MEDIA ADS

KEEP THE IDEA
WHAT SURVIVES
The bracket as the interactive element, and the tap-to-choose-your-colour state. This is the single most ownable device in the whole library — an ad that answers back.
WHAT FAILS
Three phones floating on a grey gradient with no context. The three different accent colours in one frame break the one-accent rule the system depends on. The interaction is described, never shown happening.
THE REBUILD
Hold the sequence in one frame: closed, tapped, expanded. Real hand, real thumb on glass, shallow depth. One accent across all three states so the change reads as interaction rather than decoration.
